Voice mode is the one feature that talks to the cloud - transcription uses your own OpenAI API key, and the key never leaves your device.

No cloud. No account. No crumbs.

Your expenses live on your phone and nowhere else. There's no server, no sign-up, no one peeking in the pantry.

Everything stays on your device. Expenses are stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Works offline. On a plane, in the basement, in a mouse hole - logging always works.
No account, no tracking. No sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry. Zero data collection.
Portable backups. Export your data as a file, import it anywhere. You own it.
One optional cloud connection. Voice mode sends audio to OpenAI for transcription with your own API key. Everything else is 100% offline, and the key is excluded from backups.

Open source, down to the last crumb.

Every line of Mäuse is on GitHub. Read it, fork it, build it yourself - nothing up our sleeves.
